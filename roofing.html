Roofing

Professional Roofing Services

Your roof is the first line of defense for your home. At Real Elite Contracting, we deliver roofing solutions that protect your investment and stand up to everything the Eastern Panhandle weather can throw at them. Whether you need a complete tear-off and replacement or targeted repairs, our veteran-owned crew handles every project with military precision and uncompromising quality.

We specialize in architectural shingles that provide superior durability, wind resistance, and curb appeal compared to standard three-tab shingles. Our full roof replacements include a complete tear-off of your old roofing material down to the deck, allowing us to inspect and replace any damaged sheathing before building back up with quality materials.

Every roof we install starts with proper synthetic underlayment for a reliable moisture barrier, followed by precise flashing installation around chimneys, valleys, dormers, and wall intersections to prevent leaks at the most vulnerable points. We finish with ridge vents that promote proper attic ventilation, extending the life of your shingles and helping regulate your home's temperature year-round.

From minor repairs and storm damage fixes to complete roof replacements, no job is too big or too small. We take pride in clean job sites, clear communication, and delivering results that last for decades.

What We Offer

  • Complete tear-off and replacement
