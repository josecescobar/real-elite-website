A kitchen budget starts with a decision about the room: are you replacing finishes, changing the layout, or extending the house? Each can be described as a kitchen remodel, but comparing their totals tells you little about what you will receive.

For homeowners in [Ashburn](/service-areas/ashburn-va), [Leesburg](/service-areas/leesburg-va), and [Brambleton](/service-areas/brambleton-va), the useful starting point is a measured layout and a list of what stays. This guide uses published outside benchmarks checked September 27, 2026. They are planning references, not a Real Elite price list.

## What the published figures actually cover

[HomeAdvisor's kitchen cost guide](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/kitchens/remodel-a-kitchen/), updated June 17, 2026, reports:

- **Typical range:** $14,589–$41,559
- **Average:** $26,945
- **Total makeover:** $65,000–$130,000 or more

The page still uses a 2025 heading despite its later update date. These figures are national; they do not establish an average for Loudoun County or a price for your chosen specifications.

A separate [2025 DMV report from Four Seasons Home Improvement](https://www.4seasonshome.com/resources/the-dmv-home-renovation-roi-report-2025-data-driven-insights-on-the-upgrades-that-maximize-property-value) lists $28,500 for a minor midrange kitchen and $72,000 for a major upscale kitchen. Treat those as that publisher's regional benchmarks. We have not independently reproduced its underlying dataset, and neither amount is a ceiling for a custom kitchen.

Do not average these sources together or apply an arbitrary Loudoun markup. Instead, ask bidders to price the same room and the same product schedule.

## The choices to settle before comparing quotes

**Cabinets:** name the manufacturer or construction specification, door finish, interior fittings, and installation scope. A price for refinishing existing boxes cannot be compared directly with a price for replacing them.

**Counters:** identify the material, edge, backsplash height, sink cutouts, and any waterfall panels. Make the quote clear about templating, removal, delivery, and installation.

**Appliances:** list the actual models, even if the homeowner will buy them separately. Their dimensions and installation requirements belong on the plan before cabinets are ordered.

**Layout:** mark every proposed change to a sink, range, doorway, or wall. Ask which assumptions need a site visit or professional design review before a firm price can be offered.

**The rest of the house:** specify flooring transitions, paint limits, protection of occupied rooms, and disposal. A kitchen quote that stops at the cabinet footprint may leave work you expected outside the contract.

## Permits follow the scope

Loudoun's [Residential Additions and Alterations page](https://www.loudoun.gov/5387/Residential-Additions-and-Alterations) lists an alteration building fee of 1% of construction cost plus $130 for plan review. It also requires structural details and applicable trade permits. This is not a flat fee for every kitchen remodel; ask Building and Development which application fits your work.

For a home inside an incorporated town, that county page requires an approved town zoning permit with the application. A Leesburg mailing address alone does not determine the parcel's jurisdiction.

Ask the permit office and the relevant licensed trades about plumbing, electrical, gas, and mechanical changes. Real Elite does not take electrical work; identify the separately licensed electrical provider and its scope before agreeing to the project.

A new window, exterior vent, or bump-out also deserves an association review check. See our [Brambleton, Lansdowne, and Ashburn Farm HOA guide](/blog/hoa-approval-remodels-brambleton-lansdowne-ashburn-farm-2026). Confirm the rules for your property even if most of the work is indoors.

## Questions to answer in the estimate

### Can I use the national average as my budget?

Use it as context. A quote is useful only when it describes your layout, selections, existing conditions, allowances, and exclusions. Decide which changes are essential before trying to make a design fit a headline average.

### What if the kitchen needs more floor area?

Compare the remodel with a separately scoped [home addition](/blog/home-addition-cost-loudoun-county-2026). An extension introduces a different set of site and structural questions.

### Should I choose materials before the walkthrough?

Bring preferences and appliance ideas, but keep irreversible purchases until dimensions and installation requirements are confirmed. Ask for a written process for selection changes and their price effects.
