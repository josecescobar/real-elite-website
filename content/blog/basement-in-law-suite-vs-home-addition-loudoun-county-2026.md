"In-law suite" describes a use, not a project. A parent moving in, an adult child coming home, or a long-term guest can be housed in a finished lower level, a converted garage, or a new wing off the main floor. In Loudoun County those three paths differ in price, in which county offices review them, and in which contractor license belongs on the permit.

This guide compares the basement suite with the addition. It uses published figures and county sources checked September 27, 2026. It is planning context, not a description of a completed Real Elite project or a fixed-price offer.

## Start with who will live there

Decide the need before you decide the room:

- **Daily access:** Someone who should not use stairs every day needs a main-level bedroom and bath. A basement suite does not solve that, however well it is finished.
- **Independence:** A bedroom and bath is a guest suite. Adding a kitchen changes the zoning question (see below).
- **Privacy and light:** A walkout lower level can feel like its own home. A basement with only window wells will feel like a basement.
- **Time horizon:** A suite for a few years and a suite you expect to rent or sell with the house call for different decisions about entrances and kitchens.

If the answer is "main level," compare an addition with a garage conversion. If a lower level will work, the basement is usually the smaller project, because the structure, roof, and foundation already exist.

## What the published numbers say

[EA Home Design's Loudoun additions guide](https://eahomedesign.com/home-additions-loudoun-county/), dated August 22, 2026, publishes these design-build figures:

- **Main-level bedroom and bath suite (400–600 sq ft):** $180,000–$350,000
- **Garage conversion (adds 400–500 sq ft):** $60,000–$130,000
- **Basement finish (800–1,200 sq ft):** $60,000–$180,000

EA says its table prices include design, engineering, permits, and inspections. The same page later gives $150,000–$300,000 for "a main level family room or bedroom suite," so its own figures are not perfectly consistent. We quote the table row. EA also notes that below-grade space "counts for less at appraisal and does not solve a need for main level living."

[Denny + Gardner's in-law suite article](https://www.dennyandgardner.com/blog/in-law-suite-cost-northern-va) gives about $225–$275 per square foot to convert a basement, attic, or garage, and about $300 per square foot for a new addition. That article is dated July 2021, and it discusses Fairfax County and D.C. rules, not Loudoun's. Treat it as older regional context.

Neither source is a Loudoun average, and the figures should not be averaged together. ## The basement suite: inside the house you have

A lower-level suite is mostly a question of what the basement will support:

- **Bedroom escape:** A sleeping room needs a compliant emergency escape and rescue opening. Check any existing window against the required dimensions. Our [basement bathroom and egress guide](/blog/basement-bathroom-egress-ashburn-leesburg-2026) covers the dimensions.
- **Bathroom:** Where the drain lines are, and whether the fixtures sit below the sewer line, sets much of the bathroom cost.
- **Kitchenette or kitchen:** Loudoun's [Finished Basements page](https://www.loudoun.gov/1172/Finished-Basements) says a finished basement with a personal kitchen needs a zoning permit and an additional $165 zoning fee.
- **Entrance:** A new exterior door or areaway changes the outside of the house. That brings HOA review wherever an association governs the property; see the [HOA approval guide](/blog/hoa-approval-remodels-brambleton-lansdowne-ashburn-farm-2026).

The county's basement permit fee is 1% of construction cost, excluding electrical, mechanical, plumbing, and gas, with a $65 minimum. It adds $130 for plan review when the job needs full plans. Trade permits are separate.

## The addition: new footprint on the lot

An addition starts outside. Loudoun's [Residential Additions and Alterations page](https://www.loudoun.gov/5387/Residential-Additions-and-Alterations) requires a plat for additions, showing the house, the addition, and distances to the side and rear property lines. It publishes these fees:

- **1,000 square feet or less:** $395, covering the building permit, plan review, and county zoning permit
- **More than 1,000 square feet:** 1% of construction cost, plus $335 plan review and the county zoning permit

Trade permits are separate. The same page classifies screened porches as additions, and it says plans review is required for gas permits on residential alterations and additions as of October 1, 2025.

**Well and septic.** The county says Health Department approval is required when a bedroom is added to a house served by well and septic, and to contact the Health Department before applying. The [Health Clearances page](https://www.loudoun.gov/5770/Health-Clearances) lists a $25 clearance fee per application reviewed. This matters most on western Loudoun acreage near [Purcellville](/service-areas/purcellville-va) and [Aldie](/service-areas/aldie-va). It applies to a basement bedroom too, not only to a new wing.

## Accessory dwelling rules, and the "ADU" mix-up

If the suite has its own cooking, bathroom, and sleeping space, the county zoning ordinance may treat it as an accessory dwelling. Loudoun's [Zoning Ordinance](https://www.loudoun.gov/1755/Zoning-Ordinance) was adopted and took effect December 13, 2023. Section 4.02.01 of the [ordinance text](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/loudouncounty-va-zo/) says:

- **Size in suburban districts:** The footprint cannot exceed 50% of the main house's footprint. Gross floor area cannot exceed the lesser of 50% of the main house's floor area or 1,200 square feet. Transition, rural, and JLMA districts allow the lesser of 70% or 2,500 square feet.
- **Septic:** On lots with individual sewage disposal systems, an accessory dwelling needs Health Department approval.
- **Number:** Only one accessory dwelling is allowed on a lot under 20 acres.
- **Location:** It may be inside the main house or in an accessory building. An attached one follows the yard requirements for the main structure.

The parking table lists one space per accessory dwelling, and section 3.03 requires accessory uses to stay under the same ownership as the main use. We did not find an owner-occupancy or separate-entrance rule in section 4.02.01, so do not assume one either way. Ask county zoning about your parcel before you design a separate unit.

A search for "ADU Loudoun County" brings up something else. Loudoun uses ADU for its [Affordable Dwelling Unit program](https://www.loudoun.gov/1813/Affordable-Dwelling-Unit-Program), which offers income-eligible buyers and renters newly built affordable housing. It is not a permit path for a suite in your house.

## Towns and historic districts come first

The county ordinance does not apply inside Loudoun's seven incorporated towns. For those properties, the county requires an approved town zoning permit with the building application:

- **Purcellville:** The [town's FAQ](https://purcellvilleva.gov/FAQ.aspx?QID=66) says a town zoning permit is required for any new construction, alteration, or addition. The county will not accept a building application inside town limits without it.
- **Leesburg and Middleburg:** Confirm town zoning before the county application. [Middleburg's planning page](https://www.middleburgva.gov/179/Planning-Zoning) calls for a Zoning Location Permit for any work that needs a county building permit. The Town of Leesburg's [community development FAQ](https://www.leesburgva.gov/departments/community-development/community-development-faqs) says a town zoning permit is required for almost any construction in town, including room additions and interior alterations. For [Leesburg](/service-areas/leesburg-va), check whether the parcel is actually inside town limits. The mailing address alone will not tell you.

In the Aldie historic district, the county says [most exterior changes need Historic District Review Committee approval](https://www.loudoun.gov/2370/County-Historic-Districts) before work begins, including additions. A [Certificate of Appropriateness](https://www.loudoun.gov/5163/Certificate-Of-Appropriateness) does not automatically issue a zoning permit. It expires if the work does not begin within five years.

[Ashburn](/service-areas/ashburn-va), [Brambleton](/service-areas/brambleton-va), [Lansdowne](/service-areas/lansdowne-va), and [South Riding](/service-areas/south-riding-va) are unincorporated. The county permit and your HOA's review are the two checks there.

## The license on the permit

Virginia's contractor regulations define the scopes differently. Under [18VAC50-22-30](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title18/agency50/chapter22/section30/), the Home Improvement Contractor (HIC) specialty covers repairs and improvements to existing dwellings. It "does not include new construction functions beyond the existing building structure" other than certain decks, patios, driveways, and outbuildings. It also excludes electrical, plumbing, HVAC, and gas fitting. The Residential Building Contractor (RBC) classification in [18VAC50-22-20](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title18/agency50/chapter22/section20/) covers construction of dwellings, including accessory structures.

Real Elite holds Virginia Class A contractor license 2705198604 with the HIC specialty. That fits work inside an existing house, such as a basement suite. A new wing is new construction beyond the existing structure, and the contractor on that permit needs a classification that covers it. Ask any bidder which license covers the addition and which licensed trades will pull the electrical, plumbing, and mechanical permits. Real Elite does not take electrical work. Check any license on [DPOR's license lookup](https://www.dpor.virginia.gov/LicenseLookup).

## How to choose

- **A basement suite fits** when the person can use stairs, the lower level is dry, a compliant bedroom escape is possible, and the suite doesn't need street-level entry.
- **A main-level addition fits** when step-free living is the point, the lot has room within setbacks, and the budget can carry new foundation, framing, and roof.
- **A garage conversion is worth pricing** when you need a level entry but not new footprint.

What we won't promise: that one option adds more resale value. EA's appraisal note and Denny + Gardner's value comment are those firms' views. They don't measure your house.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a basement suite with a kitchen count as a second dwelling?

It may. The ordinance defines a dwelling unit by its independent cooking, sanitation, and sleeping facilities. Ask county zoning before you add a kitchen, and expect the $165 zoning fee for a personal kitchen in a finished basement.

### Can I add a bedroom on a septic lot?

Only with Health Department approval, and the county says to get it before you apply for permits. Bring your septic permit records to the first meeting.

### Which should I price first?

Price the one that meets the need. If both do, get a basement walkthrough first. It will show whether moisture, ceiling height, or egress rules out the cheaper path.
