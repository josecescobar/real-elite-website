The phrase “covered patio” can mean a roof over a paved area, a raised deck with a roof, or an enclosed room connected to the house. Before asking what it costs, describe which of those you want and how you expect to use it.

For outdoor projects in [Ashburn](/service-areas/ashburn-va), [Brambleton](/service-areas/brambleton-va), [Lansdowne](/service-areas/lansdowne-va), and [Leesburg](/service-areas/leesburg-va), the roof, enclosure, lot, and association requirements belong in the first discussion.

## Published outdoor-living benchmarks

The [2025 DMV report from Four Seasons Home Improvement](https://www.4seasonshome.com/resources/the-dmv-home-renovation-roi-report-2025-data-driven-insights-on-the-upgrades-that-maximize-property-value), checked September 27, 2026, lists:

- **Wood deck addition:** $19,000
- **Composite deck addition:** $25,000
- **Screened porch:** $28,000
- **Three-season sunroom:** $35,000
- **Four-season sunroom:** $50,000

These are that publisher's regional figures. We have not reproduced its underlying cost dataset. They are not a local quote, a per-square-foot rate, or a definition of what your structure will include.

For another reference, [EA Home Design's Loudoun guide](https://eahomedesign.com/home-additions-loudoun-county/) lists $60,000–$150,000 for a four-season sunroom of 200–350 square feet. That is an enclosed-room benchmark with a different scope.

Neither source establishes a specific Loudoun covered-patio range. A roofed patio does not automatically cost somewhere between their deck and sunroom figures. Obtain a design and estimate for the structure you intend to build.

## Describe the whole outdoor room

Start with a plan showing how people will move between the house, seating, cooking area, and yard. Then ask the estimate to identify:

- The footprint and whether the structure is attached or freestanding.
- The proposed foundation and support design, including any assessment of an existing patio or deck.
- Roof shape, connection to the house if any, and water discharge.
- Ceiling, posts, trim, flooring or decking, rails, stairs, and screens.
- Any separately scoped equipment, lighting, outlets, fans, or gas appliances.
- Removal, protection of adjacent landscaping, and restoration included in the price.

Real Elite does not take electrical work. Identify the separately licensed electrical provider for that portion of an outdoor-living project, and clarify other trade responsibilities before the scope is agreed.

## The deck permit path has limits

Loudoun's [Decks page](https://www.loudoun.gov/1166/Decks) requires building and zoning permits. Its Typical Deck Detail is limited to qualifying residential, single-level decks conforming to the packet; roofs, screened porches, hot tubs, gazebos, and detached decks are excluded. A small footprint alone does not make a roofed project eligible.

For decks **under 1,000 square feet outside an incorporated town**, that page lists $265 for a qualifying typical deck and $395 for a deck built to submitted plans. These include county zoning; equipment-related permits can add fees. At 1,000 square feet or above, different calculations apply. Town properties have a separate zoning path.

The county classifies screened porches as additions. See the [addition cost guide](/blog/home-addition-cost-loudoun-county-2026) for that published fee schedule. Ask Building and Development to classify your actual roofed-patio design rather than assuming the full-plan deck fee applies to every outdoor roof.

## Check the association before buying finishes

The [HOA approval guide](/blog/hoa-approval-remodels-brambleton-lansdowne-ashburn-farm-2026) links to association sources and explains what to confirm. Obtain the current application and written decision for the specific property. A neighbor's existing deck is a useful design reference, but it does not establish approval for your materials or footprint.

Keep the approved drawing and product descriptions with the estimate. If the association asks for a different railing or roof finish, reconcile that change with the priced scope before ordering.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can the existing patio support a new roof?

Do not assume so from its appearance. Ask for the proposed support and foundation design to be checked before treating the existing surface as reusable structural support.

### Can I compare bids by square footage alone?

Only after aligning the scope. A roof, stairs, screening, and restoration can be included in one price and absent from another. Compare the drawings and exclusions alongside the total.

### What about an outdoor kitchen?

The checked sources do not establish a reliable Loudoun-specific range for that scope. List the equipment, counters, utilities, and installation separately so it does not disappear inside a generic deck allowance.
