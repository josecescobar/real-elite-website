Custom Outbuildings Done Right

Purpose-built structures with the same quality craftsmanship we bring to every project.

Whether you need extra storage, a workshop, or a custom outbuilding to complement your property, Real Elite Contracting builds it from the ground up with quality materials and expert craftsmanship. Every shed and outbuilding we construct is designed to be durable, functional, and built to match the look of your home.

We handle every phase of the build — from the initial layout and foundation work through framing, siding, roofing, trim, and finishing touches like ramps and custom doors. Our lean-to and shed designs are tailored to your specific needs, whether you're looking for a simple storage solution or a fully finished outbuilding.

What We Build

  • Custom sheds sized to your specifications
  • Lean-to structures and covered storage areas
  • Full-size outbuildings and storage buildings
  • Proper structural framing for long-term durability
  • Siding installation to match your home's exterior
  • Roofing with architectural shingles and proper flashing
  • Access ramps for easy equipment and storage entry
