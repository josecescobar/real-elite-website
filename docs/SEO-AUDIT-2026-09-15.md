# SEO Audit — realelitecontracting.com

**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Sources:** OpenSEO (crawl, backlinks, live SERPs, GBP profile + reviews), Google Search Console, Windsor.ai (GA4, Google Business Profile performance), plus direct reading of this repository.
**Client-facing report:** https://claude.ai/artifact/M5No9zGaKngJuK5ArFgbAP

## Verdict

The site is technically clean and commercially misaimed.

The OpenSEO crawl of 50 pages returned **zero issues**: every page 200, indexable, in the sitemap, with hand-written titles and meta descriptions and correct structured data. There is nothing to repair.

The problem is targeting. Over 28 days (2026-08-16 → 2026-09-13) Search Console recorded **10,785 impressions and 27 clicks** (0.25% CTR) at an average position of roughly 22. Two root causes, one in the Google Business Profile and one in this codebase.

> **Correction (2026-09-16).** This section first reported "7,917 impressions and 4 clicks (0.05% CTR)". That was wrong — the click figure came from a truncated query-dimension pull rather than a site total. Recomputed from the daily series, the real 28-day figures are the ones above, and an independent GA4/GSC pull the following morning agreed (24 clicks, 9,441 impressions, 0.25% CTR on a window one day later). The corrected CTR is five times the original claim. The direction of every finding below is unchanged — 0.25% at position 22 is still poor — but the severity was overstated.

## Finding 1 — Google reads this business as a deck builder, not a roofer

The GBP (`cid 13647109271157863950`) carries `roofing_contractor` as a category but:

- **14 service items are listed. None is roofing.** They are all Remodeler / General Contractor items (deck construction, drywall, exterior painting, flooring…).
- **All 6 reviews describe decks, a fence, or porch stone.** Not one mentions a roof.
- **Google's own derived `place_topics` are:** `deck` (4), `wood` (2), `deck replacement` (2).
- **No paving service items and no paving category**, despite 10 paving pages on the site.

Consequence, verified against live SERPs: for `roof replacement estimate charles town wv` the local pack is Orion Exteriors, Jade Roofing, Titan Roofing. Real Elite is absent from the pack and appears only as an organic link below an AI Overview, the 3-pack, and People Also Ask.

**GBP performance, 90 days to 2026-09-14 (Windsor.ai):**

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Search impressions (desktop + mobile) | 163 |
| Maps impressions (desktop + mobile) | 99 |
| **Total impressions** | **262** |
| Website clicks | 13 |
| Call clicks | 2 |
| Messages | 0 |

**Action (no code):** add roofing service items (roof replacement, roof repair, storm damage roof repair, roof inspection, shingle/metal roof installation, gutter installation) and paving items + category; run a review push targeted specifically at roofing customers, asking them to name the trade and the town.

## Finding 2 — Every service+city page targets a market we do not rank in

`src/lib/service-city-content.ts` exports `CONTENT`, keyed <code>&#96;${FeaturedServiceSlug}-${ExpansionCitySlug}&#96;</code>. It holds **62 combinations, all of them VA/MD**, because `EXPANSION_SERVICE_AREAS` in `src/lib/constants.ts` contains **no West Virginia cities at all**.

So the five WV home-turf cities in `PRIMARY_SERVICE_AREAS` — Martinsburg, Inwood, Charles Town, Ranson, Hedgesville — can never produce a `/services/{service}/{city}` page. They only have the generic `/service-areas/{slug}` page.

This is exactly backwards relative to demand:

| Market group | Queries | Impressions | Clicks | Avg position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| WV home turf (0 service+city pages) | 133 | 2,354 | 1 | — |
| Distant VA luxury markets (62 pages) | 239 | 2,248 | 0 | 31.3 |
| Charles Town, roofing only | 24 | 831 | 0 | 11.8 |
| Martinsburg, roofing only | 30 | 744 | 1 | 30.7 |

These rows are sums over named-market queries, not site totals — together they cover roughly 43% of the site's impressions. The comparison between them holds; do not read the clicks column as a sitewide figure.

Individual Charles Town roofing queries rank in the top 3 with zero clicks:

| Query | Impressions | Clicks | Avg position |
|---|---|---|---|
| roof replacement estimate charles town wv | 67 | 0 | 1.9 |
| charles town wv roof replacement cost | 63 | 0 | 2.1 |
| average cost of a new roof charles town wv | 72 | 0 | 2.3 |
| new roof cost charles town wv | 58 | 0 | 2.6 |
| charles town wv roof replacement estimate | 56 | 0 | 2.8 |
| roof replacement cost martinsburg wv | 95 | 0 | 6.8 |
| free storm damage roof inspection | 185 | 0 | 13.6 |

**Action (code):** extend the service+city generation to cover WV primary areas. Highest value first: `roofing × {charles-town-wv, martinsburg-wv}`, then `decks × {charles-town-wv, martinsburg-wv}`.

Note the type constraint: `CONTENT` is keyed on `ExpansionCitySlug`. Covering WV means either widening that union to include the primary WV slugs or introducing a separate home-turf content map. The `generateStaticParams` + `dynamicParams = false` pattern in `src/app/services/[service]/[city]/page.tsx` already guards against soft 404s, so adding keys is safe once the types allow it.

## Finding 3 — A blog post is absorbing commercial intent

`/blog/roof-replacement-cost-eastern-panhandle-2026` took **1,919 impressions and 2 clicks** (0.1%). It is the page ranking for `roof replacement estimate charles town wv`, a hire-me query.

The article answers the price question, which is also what the AI Overview above it does. Verified: both money SERPs checked open with an `ai_overview` block at rank 1.

**Action:** keep the article, add a top-of-page estimate CTA, and cross-link it to the WV roofing pages once they exist. Steer new content toward questions an answer box cannot close (crew process, real claim walkthroughs, what the written estimate contains) rather than more cost guides.

## What is already right

- Crawl: 50/50 pages 200, indexable, in sitemap, zero audit issues.
- Structured data in use: `GeneralContractor` (13), `Service` (8), `FAQPage` (3), `BreadcrumbList`, `AggregateRating`, `Article`.
- Response times mostly under 300ms.
- `next-sitemap.config.js` deliberately excludes metadata routes and internal tools (`/review-request`, `/sales`) — keeps Search Console coverage clean.
- `dynamicParams = false` on the service+city route prevents soft 404s.
- Referring domains grew 17 → 31 over the trailing year.
- GBP rating 5.0 with an owner reply on every review.

---

## Recommended order of work

1. **GBP service items + roofing review push.** No code, largest leverage, unblocks local pack eligibility.
2. **WV service+city pages**, roofing first, Charles Town and Martinsburg.
3. **Estimate CTA + cross-links** on the roof cost article.
4. **Strengthen `/storm-damage`** around "free storm damage roof inspection" (185 impressions, position 13.6, the single most-shown query).
5. **Freeze new VA luxury-market pages** until local signals support them.
