Deck Construction

Custom-Built Composite & Wood Decks — Designed for Your Lifestyle

Expert Deck Building from Start to Finish

At Real Elite Contracting, we build decks that stand the test of time. Whether you're looking for a low-maintenance composite deck or a classic wood deck, our veteran-owned team handles every phase of construction with precision and care — from initial framing to the final finishing touches.

Our comprehensive deck construction services include:

  • Composite & Wood Decking: We work with premium composite materials and pressure-treated lumber to deliver the perfect look and durability for your home.
  • Structural Framing: Every deck starts with a solid foundation. Our framing is engineered for strength, safety, and longevity.
  • Railings & Balusters: From sleek white vinyl railings to traditional wood designs, we install railing systems that complement your deck's style.
  • Stairs & Steps: Custom-built stairs with proper rise and run for safe, comfortable access to your yard.
  • Finishing & Details: Kickboard skirting, solar post cap lights, trim work, and all the details that make a deck feel complete.
  • Before & After Transformations: We specialize in tearing out old, worn-out decks and replacing them with beautiful new outdoor living spaces.

Proudly serving homeowners throughout WV's Eastern Panhandle — including Martinsburg, Berkeley County, Jefferson County, and Morgan County — as well as Frederick County, VA and Washington County, MD. As a veteran-owned business, we bring military discipline and integrity to every project.
